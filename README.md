## Business context

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a cloud-based computing platform that offers a wide range of services to businesses and individuals. It is one of the most widely used cloud computing platforms in the world. It is designed to help businesses and individuals store, manage, and process data, applications, and services in the cloud, without the need for on-premises infrastructure.

AWS Cloud Computing is characterized by its ability to scale, its ease of use, and its affordability. AWS offers a variety of cloud computing services, including computing power, storage, and databases, as well as analytics, networking, and security services. These services are available on-demand, which means that users can pay for them as they use them, without any upfront investment or long-term commitment.

One of the primary benefits of AWS Cloud Computing is its scalability. AWS can quickly and easily scale up or down based on user demand, which means that businesses can quickly and easily respond to changing market conditions. AWS is also highly reliable and secure, with multiple layers of security and redundancy built in to protect against data loss and downtime.
